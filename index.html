Who I am

About Me

I'm a creative professional who believes good design and a good voice do the same job: they make people feel something. I combine visual identity, branding and social media design with voice over to tell stories that are clear, warm and memorable.

My approach starts with listening — understanding the message and the audience — then shaping it into visuals and sound that communicate with intention. Design gives a story its look; voice gives it its heartbeat.

Design is how a story looks. Voice is how it feels.

My goal is to help clients and brands communicate with clarity and personality, with work that is thoughtful, consistent and a pleasure to collaborate on.
